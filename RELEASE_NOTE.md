# Fakeplayer 0.4.1-hotfix

## Fixes

- Fixed fake players being impossible to attack even when invulnerability was disabled. Fake players are now treated as client-loaded and no longer remain in the dimension-change state after teleporting, and `/fp test` verifies that a fake player can take damage.
